KNOW WHAT YOU’RE LOOKING AT

More information. Clear limits.

01

Recorded lighting

Streetlight records show coverage. They don’t tell us how bright a street is or whether a lamp works tonight.

02

Historical activity

Pedestrian patterns describe past observations. They aren’t live crowds or a measure of safety.

03

Confidence, explained

Missing evidence stays visible. Route preference scores don’t predict danger, and walking times are estimates.
